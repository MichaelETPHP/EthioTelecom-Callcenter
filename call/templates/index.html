SMS API documentation

Send SMS from any backend through your own Android phone and SIM. This guide takes you from phone registration to a production request.

REST API

How it works

Your projectCalls the REST API from a secure backend.
→
SMS platformAuthenticates and queues your message.
→
Your Android phoneSends through your SIM and carrier.
Important: the platform is free to use, but your mobile carrier may charge your SIM for each SMS. The recipient sees your SIM number as the sender.

Your phone, your credentials

Do not place your password or token in React, mobile web, or other browser code. Send requests from your backend and keep secrets in environment variables.

The username and password are created when you register your own Android phone. They identify your account; the connected SIM number becomes the SMS sender.

SMS_API_USER=YOUR_GENERATED_LOGIN
SMS_API_PASSWORD=YOUR_GENERATED_PASSWORD

Unauthorized?

Use the account login and password returned when the phone was first registered. Your SIM phone number is the sender, not the API username.

UsernameRegistration response: loginUsually a generated six-character uppercase ID. Do not enter the phone number.
PasswordRegistration response: passwordUse the exact generated value. It is only returned for a new user.
SIM numberSMS sender onlyThis is the number recipients see. It is not used for API authentication.
Test credentials before sending A correct login returns your registered devices. A wrong login returns HTTP 401.
StatusMeaningFix
401Missing or incorrect credentialsUse the generated login and password with Basic auth.
400Invalid request or phone numberUse E.164 numbers such as +251911234567 and include message text.
503No eligible online deviceOpen the Android app, allow SMS permission, and disable battery optimization.

Production checklist

SecretsStore SMS_API_USER and SMS_API_PASSWORD only on your server.
ValidationConvert recipients to E.164 format and reject empty messages before calling the API.
DeliverySave the returned message ID and query GET /messages/{id} for status.
Phone uptimeKeep the Android phone online with SMS permission and battery optimization disabled.
Failure handlingRetry temporary network and 503 failures with exponential backoff.
ConsentSend only to recipients who agreed to receive messages and follow carrier rules.
